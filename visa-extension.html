Ensuring Your Continued Legal Stay in India

At Startup Solicitors, we provide professional and proactive visa extension services for foreign nationals residing in India. An expired visa can lead to serious legal consequences, including fines, detention, and deportation. Our immigration experts ensure that your extension application is filed correctly and well in advance of the expiry date, providing you with uninterrupted legal status and peace of mind.

We handle extensions for all types of long-term visas, including Employment, Student, Medical, and Dependent (X) visas. Our team manages the entire process through the e-FRRO portal, from compiling the necessary documentation to liaising with the authorities for a timely decision.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. When should I apply for a visa extension?
It is highly recommended to apply for a visa extension at least 60 days before your current visa expires. The application must be filed before the visa's expiry date to avoid being considered an overstay.
2. Can I travel outside India while my extension application is pending?
It is generally not advisable to travel internationally while your extension application is under process with the FRRO, as this can lead to complications. If travel is unavoidable, specific permissions may be required.
3. What are the consequences of overstaying my visa?
Overstaying a visa is a serious offense in India. It can result in financial penalties, difficulty in getting future visas for India, and in severe cases, detention and deportation.
4. Do I need a valid Residential Permit to apply for an extension?
Yes, a valid Residential Permit (RP), obtained during your initial FRRO registration, is a mandatory document for applying for a visa extension. The RP is extended along with the visa.
