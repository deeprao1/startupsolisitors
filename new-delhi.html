Common Questions About Company Registration & Legal Services in New Delhi

Get answers to the most frequently asked questions about company registration, business setup, legal compliance, and expert advisory in New Delhi.

How long does the company registration process typically take in New Delhi?
While timelines can vary, the company registration process in New Delhi can typically be completed within 7-12 business days, assuming all documentation is correctly prepared and submitted. Our team works diligently to expedite the process and avoid unnecessary delays.
What are the main differences between a Private Limited Company and an LLP for a startup in New Delhi?
A Private Limited Company is often preferred for startups seeking equity funding, as it allows for the issuance of shares. It has a more formal governance structure. An LLP, on the other hand, offers operational flexibility and has fewer compliance requirements, making it a good choice for professional and service-oriented businesses. We can help you analyze your business model to determine the best fit.
Can foreign nationals or NRIs own 100% of a business in New Delhi?
Yes, in most sectors, India's Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) policy permits 100% ownership by foreign nationals or Non-Resident Indians (NRIs). We specialize in advising international clients on FDI regulations and ensuring their business setup in New Delhi is fully compliant with Indian laws.
What documents are required for company registration in New Delhi?
For a private limited company registration in New Delhi, the primary company registration documents required include: PAN cards, identity proofs (like Aadhaar card or passport), and address proofs for all directors and shareholders. Additionally, you'll need proof of the registered office address (like a rental agreement or utility bill) and an NOC from the property owner. Our team will provide a detailed checklist.
What is the estimated cost of LLP registration in New Delhi?
The cost of LLP registration in New Delhi is generally lower than that of a private limited company. The total cost depends on factors like the number of partners and the contribution amount. Our packages are all-inclusive, covering government fees, stamp duty, and our professional charges. Contact us for a precise quote based on your requirements.
Can you provide a legal compliance checklist for a startup in New Delhi?
Absolutely. A basic legal compliance checklist for a startup in New Delhi includes: company incorporation, PAN/TAN registration, GST registration, opening a current bank account, and ensuring annual ROC filings (AOC-4 and MGT-7). Depending on your industry, you may also need Shop & Establishment registration, a Professional Tax license, and other permits. We provide a tailored checklist as part of our legal services in New Delhi.
