Payment Gateway & Aggregator Licensing

Home / FinTech & Banking / Payment Gateway & Aggregator Licensing

Startup Solicitors LLP offers specialized legal and compliance services for obtaining Payment Gateway (PG) and Payment Aggregator (PA) licenses from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). Our team provides end-to-end support, from initial consultation to final license approval, ensuring your FinTech business meets all regulatory requirements.

Navigating the RBI's stringent guidelines for payment systems can be complex. We simplify the process by handling all documentation, compliance checks, and communication with the regulatory authorities, allowing you to focus on building your payment solutions.

  • RBI Liaison: Acting as your representative in all communications with the RBI.
  • Post-License Compliance: Ongoing support to ensure adherence to regulatory reporting.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Who needs a Payment Aggregator license in India?
Any entity that facilitates e-commerce sites and merchants to accept various payment instruments from customers for completion of their payment obligations without the need for merchants to create a separate payment integration system of their own requires a PA license.
2. What is the minimum net worth for a Payment Aggregator?
As per RBI guidelines, a Payment Aggregator must have a minimum net worth of ₹15 crore at the time of application and should achieve a net worth of ₹25 crore by the end of the third financial year.
